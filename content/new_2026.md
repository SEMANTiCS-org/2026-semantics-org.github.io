# What’s new in 2026? Evolving with Our Community

SEMANTiCS continues to thrive thanks to the long-standing commitment of our researchers and practitioners. To better support the high volume of quality work we receive and to ensure every contribution finds its ideal stage, we are introducing a few thoughtful updates to this year's program:

- **The Blue Sky Track**: We are introducing a dedicated space for visionary, long-term research that pushes the boundaries of our field.

- **Enhanced Visibility through Posters & Demos**: To ensure greater interaction, the Research & Innovation and Industry & Use Case tracks now include a "fast-track" option to the Posters and Demos session. This provides authors with more flexible ways to showcase their work to the community.

- **Expanded Project Hosting**: The Posters and Demos track has been expanded to host recently accepted Project Proposals, providing a home for the very latest initiatives in our ecosystem.

- **Dedicated ORKG Track**: The Open Research Knowledge Graph (ORKG) submissions have moved from the individual calls to a dedicated track.
