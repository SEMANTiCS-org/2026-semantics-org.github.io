# DBpedia Day 2026

SEMANTiCS 2026 will host the **DBpedia Day** on 15 September 2026 in Ghent, Belgium.

## Community Meeting

[DBpedia](https://www.dbpedia.org/) is an international crowd-sourced community effort to extract structured information from Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons and to make this information available on the Web as Linked Open Data.

DBpedia provides tools that allow you to create, maintain, improve, integrate and use KGs to build applications, e.g. BBC has created the World Cup 2010 website by interconnecting textual content and facts from their knowledge base. Data provided by DBpedia was greatly involved in creating this knowledge graph.

DBpedia has become a high-impact, high-visibility project because of our foundation in excellent Knowledge Engineering as the pivot point between scientific methods, innovation and industrial-grade output. The drivers behind DBpedia are 4 out of the [TOP 10 Most Influential Scholars](https://www.aminer.org/ai2000/ke) in Knowledge Engineering and the C-level executives of our members.

As one of the pioneering open Knowledge Graph initiatives, DBpedia has been connecting structured knowledge since 2007. This year's DBpedia Day will bring together researchers, developers, practitioners, and organizations to discuss the future of Knowledge Graphs, AI, and the technologies that power them.

## This year's topics

- New and established Knowledge Graphs
- Knowledge Graph engineering and maintenance
- AI-assisted Knowledge Graph construction
- Entity linking, integration, and interoperability
- Querying, reasoning, visualization, and analytics
- Real-world applications, success stories, and lessons learned

This year's theme particularly explores how Knowledge Graphs and Large Language Models can complement each other—from AI-assisted KG engineering to using KGs for more reliable, explainable, and interoperable AI. Please find more information at the [Call for Presentations.](https://www.dbpedia.org/blog/dbpedia-day-2026/)
