SEMANTiCS is looking into the future! This special track, sponsored by the prestigious CCC, is looking for visionary ideas, long term challenges, and opportunities in the Semantic Web and Knowledge Graph research field. Concretely, we invite submissions focusing, among others, on one of the following foci:

- **Solution**: present groundbreaking solution concepts or paradigms to long-term, well-known (conceptual or technical) challenges in the field;
- **Problem**: clearly describe and specify a major challenge that, once solved, would have a significant impact on the research community, alike to the “millenium problems” in mathematics;
- **Application**: describe a relevant application problem from industry to be solved with knowledge graphs and semantic technologies;
- **Cross-disciplinary research**: identify emerging and promising cross-disciplinary research ideas; argue for promising paradigms to be learned and adapted from other research disciplines; discuss the potential impact of knowledge graphs on other research disciplines;
- **Societal aspects**: present ideas and visions of contributing to solving societal challenges such as climate change, safeguarding democracy in the digital world, etc.

Since submissions to the track are supposed to describe open questions and unexplored ideas, they will naturally take a different shape than standard research papers. Despite that, they are expected to follow the same scientific rigor as submissions to the other tracks, i.e., demonstrate the relevance of the problem, provide an overview on the state of the art (and/or document a lack thereof), underpin claims with references, present well-funded arguments etc.

Potential topics to be addressed include but are not limited to:

- Neurosymbolic AI
- Knowledge graphs and language models
- Human-AI interaction and knowledge graphs
- Semantics and distributed ledgers
- Knowledge graphs and robotics
- Hybrid intelligence
- Spatio-temporal and multi-modal knowledge graphs
- Graph foundation models
- Knowledge‑driven data quality & governance
- Trust‑weighted and uncertainty‑aware knowledge graphs
- Cognitive knowledge graphs
- On‑device / edge knowledge processing
- Self‑maintaining / self‑healing knowledge graphs
- Causal knowledge graphs
- The impact of AI agents and agentic systems on knowledge graphs
- Quantum computing and knowledge graphs
- Brain-knowledge graph interfaces

## Reviews and Review Criteria

Submissions will be judged on the extent to which they expand the possibilities and horizons of the field or challenge existing assumptions prevalent in the field. Depending on the number of submissions, each paper will be reviewed by at least three committee members, and the final ranking will be established through consensus in the program committee panel. The review criteria will focus on the level to which the papers:

1. contribute provocative and out of the box propositions,
2. propose high-risk/high-gain ideas,
3. differ from the mainstream research and
4. address a long-term time horizon till the foreseen adoption by the research community.

All accepted papers will be presented at the conference in two different modes: the three best submissions will be presented in the main conference program, further accepted papers will be showcased as posters.

## Awards

To encourage researchers to present truly visionary concepts, the CCC is offering prizes for up to 3 top papers in this special track: first prize $1000, second prize $750, and third prize $500, to be awarded as travel grants. These prizes will be granted to the winners selected by the Program Committee.
The awards will be given based on public voting among the three best submissions at the conference.

## Submissions

Submissions to this track should follow the same formatting guidelines as submissions to the research track, but are limited to at most EIGHT pages in length (excluding references) for both submission and final publication. The three finalist papers will be published in the main conference proceedings (IOS Press); the other accepted submissions will be published in CEUR together with the poster and demo papers. All submissions should be sent via [EasyChair](https://easychair.org/conferences?conf=semantics2026).
