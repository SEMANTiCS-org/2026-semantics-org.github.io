# SEMANTiCS is also about Community Building

##### March 31, 2026 by SEMANTiCS

<img src="../img/news/2026_03_31.png" style="" width="100%" height="auto" alt="">

The research ecosystem is inherently dynamic and diverse. As researchers progress through their careers, their interests evolve, shaped by their environment, scholarship, and professional networks. Consequently, research communities are rarely static. They are living environments in which researchers join, gravitate towards new ideas, and move between disciplines as their work demands.

The SEMANTiCS community is no exception. This fluidity raises a vital question:

**How do we ensure that SEMANTiCS remains both attractive and hospitable over time, staying truly open to newcomers?**

To remain attractive, we strive to create a top-tier environment where researchers can discuss the latest advances in the field. To remain hospitable, we work to ensure that every participant feels empowered to share their work. Our goal is to lower barriers and dismantle the hidden walls that can too often stand in the way of a genuine sense of belonging. We are encouraged by the experiences of early-career researchers who joined us at SEMANTiCS 2025, and whose reflections highlight the importance of creating an inclusive and welcoming environment.

Sara Todorovikj, a PhD student and research assistant in Computer Science at the Chemnitz University of Technology, Germany, Sara’s work focuses on cognitive modelling, knowledge representation, and reasoning. She is currently exploring context-dependent reasoning to develop more usable and interpretable semantic technologies.

These testimonials reinforce our belief that we are on the right path. They show that building a welcoming and supportive environment is not only a goal, but something that is already being experienced by members of our community.

This is the core of our Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion mission. We want to ensure that everyone, regardless of career stage, background, or gender, has an equal opportunity to share and learn. We recognise that this is a continuous journey. As General Chairs, we are committed to learning through this process, listening to our attendees, and ensuring that our decisions prioritise the well-being of our research community. Some of the strategic initiatives we have recently undertaken include reduced registration fees for students to improve accessibility, as well as active fundraising to support travel grants for students who would otherwise be unable to attend and present their work.

SEMANTiCS is more than a conference: it is a community shaped by exchange, openness, and the shared ambition to advance semantic technologies. The field itself depends on bringing together diverse perspectives, disciplines, and experiences. That is why inclusion is not separate from SEMANTiCS; it is fundamental to what the conference is and what it can become.

As we look ahead to SEMANTiCS 2026, we want to continue building on this foundation. We aim to foster a space where cutting-edge ideas in semantic technologies can flourish, and where all participants feel they belong and can contribute with confidence. By investing in accessibility, openness, and community, we hope to ensure that SEMANTiCS remains a place where research excellence and inclusion go hand in hand.

We look forward to welcoming both returning participants and first-time attendees to our growing community.
