# Visa Information for SEMANTiCS 2026

The **22nd International Conference on Semantic Systems (SEMANTiCS 2026)** will be held in **Ghent, Belgium** from **15 to 17 September 2026**.

Depending on your nationality, you may need a visa to enter Belgium (Schengen Area). It is the sole responsibility of the attendee to investigate visa requirements and secure the necessary entry documents. We strongly recommend starting the application process **at least 2 to 3 months** prior to the conference.



## Do I Need a Visa?

Belgium is a member of the **Schengen Area**.

* **EU/EEA/Swiss Citizens:** Do not require a visa to enter Belgium.
* **Visa-Exempt Third Countries:** Citizens of certain countries (e.g., USA, Canada, Japan, Australia, UK) can enter the Schengen Area for business or tourism for up to 90 days without a visa.
* **Visa-Required Third Countries:** Citizens of all other countries must apply for a **Short-stay Schengen Visa (Type C)**.


You can check the official visa requirements and your specific country profile via the [Immigration Office](https://dofi.ibz.be/en).



## Visa Invitation Letters

The SEMANTiCS 2026 organising committee can provide visa invitation letters to registered attendees (authors, speakers, or general participants) who require one for their visa application.

### Request Process

To obtain a visa invitation letter, please ensure you complete the following steps:

1. **Register for the Conference:** You must have a valid, paid registration confirmation before requesting a letter.
2. **Submit Your Request:** Send an email to *semantics2026@easychair.org* with the subject line *"[SEMANTiCS 2026] Visa Letter Request - [Your Name]"*.
3. **Provide Required Information:** Please include the following details exactly as they appear on your passport:
* Full Name (First Name, Last Name)
* Nationality
* Affiliation / Institution
* Postal Address
* Title of your accepted paper/abstract (if applicable)
* Registration details
