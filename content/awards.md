# Awards

The SEMANTiCS program is committed to delivering cutting-edge content, featuring high-quality papers, industry applications, workshops, tutorials on recent advances, and research showcases (posters and demos). In every edition, we honour excellence in the field by presenting awards to the top-notch research contributions displayed at the conference. This year’s programme features 11 awards, which will be conferred during the closing ceremony on 17 September.

## Research and Innovation Track

1.  **Best Research Paper**: This honours the most significant research contribution presented at the conference.
2.  **Best Student Paper**: This recognises the most outstanding paper primarily authored by a student, where the student is also the corresponding author.

## Industry and Use Case Presentation Track

1.  **Best Industry and Use Case Presentation**: This celebrates the most impactful real-world use case of semantic technologies.

## Poster and Demo Track

1.  **Best Poster**: This is for the poster presentation that best conveys a novel idea or technical advancement.
2.  **Best Demo**: This recognises the most innovative live demonstration of a semantic system or tool.

## Blue Sky Track

1.  **Best Blue Sky Paper**: This honours the most visionary, groundbreaking, or high-potential long-term idea, awarded with **$1,000**.
2.  **Second Best Blue Sky Paper**: This recognises the second-ranked paper outlining a transformative future direction, with the recipient receiving **$750**.
3.  **Third Best Blue Sky Paper**: This is for the third-ranked paper that explores a promising, non-traditional research topic, which includes a prize of **$500**.

The Blue Sky Track awards are sponsored by the **Computing Community Consortium** (CCC). Read the [CCC blog post](https://cccblog.org/2026/02/20/call-for-papers-ccc-sponsored-blue-sky-track-at-semantics-2026/).

## ORKG Content Track

1.  **Best ORKG Comparison**: This celebrates the top contribution using the ORKG to provide a structured comparison, and includes a cash prize of **€250**.
2.  **Best ORKG Review/List**: This is for the most valuable ORKG contribution that synthesises knowledge into a review or list, carrying a prize of **€100**. 
3.  **Best ORKG Software/Dataset/Paper**: This recognises the most effective ORKG contribution for structuring software, datasets, or an academic paper, with the recipient receiving a prize of **€50**. 

The ORKG Content Track awards are sponsored by the [Open Research Knowledge Graph](https://orkg.org/).
