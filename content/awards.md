# Awards and Prizes

The SEMANTiCS program is committed to delivering cutting-edge content, featuring high-quality papers, industry applications, workshops, tutorials on recent advances, and research showcases (posters and demos). In every edition, we honour excellence in the field by presenting awards to the top-notch research contributions displayed at the conference. This year’s programme features 11 awards, which will be conferred during the closing ceremony on 17 September.

### How to apply

Submit an original paper to the [Blue Sky Track](../page/cfp_bluesky) presenting a visionary, unconventional, and high-potential research idea. Your submission should clearly outline the proposed direction, its novelty, and its possible long-term impact. All accepted papers in this track will be eligible for the Blue Sky Track awards.

### How to apply

Submit a contribution to the [ORKG Content Track](../page/cfp_orkg) through the Open Research Knowledge Graph (ORKG). Contributions may take the form of a structured comparison, a review or list, or a structured representation of software, datasets, or a paper. All accepted submissions in this track will be eligible for the ORKG Content Track awards.
