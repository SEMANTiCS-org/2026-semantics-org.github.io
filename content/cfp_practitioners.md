## Objectives

The Practitioners Track serves as a vibrant venue at SEMANTiCS 2026 for presenting cutting-edge innovations that bridge semantics, interactive systems, and AI. It provides researchers and practitioners a space to showcase their operational systems to the SEMANTiCS community and gather expert insights. Furthermore, this initiative acts as a valuable channel for obtaining feedback on tools that utilize human-in-the-loop evaluations, an approach that has gained significant traction in recent years within semantic web conferences.

Authors with accepted submissions to the conference tracks are especially encouraged to contribute, as this track offers an additional platform to showcase their systems. Note that an accepted contribution in the Practitioner Track does not count towards the one-author-registration-per-paper requirement for the main conference proceedings.

## Types of Contributions

Proposed contributions may include interactive systems across various platforms (standalone, web-based, mobile, VR/AR, etc.). Relevant areas include, but are not limited to:

  * Human-AI collaborative environments
  * LLM-based and Generative AI semantic technologies
  * RAG systems
  * Agentic AI and (multi)agent systems
  * Semantic-based data-driven and analytics-based user interfaces

Submitted systems must have implemented the functional features necessary to demonstrate the primary semantic benefits outlined in the submission.

## Submission Guidelines

Submissions can be made through the SEMANTiCS 2026 EasyChair link. Authors must provide the following information on the submission form:

1.  **Title of the contribution.**
2.  **Authors' list**. Including affiliation and contact information.
3.  **Abstract**.
4.  **Keywords**.
5.  **User Experience Section**. An overview of key benefits for the semantics community, user experience, and expected feedback gained from the IE session.
6.  **System availability statement**. Specify whether the tool is publicly accessible (e.g., open-source repo, app store, MVP) or limited to on-site demonstration, as reviewers and attendees often want to know if they can test the tool themselves. Authors are strongly encouraged to include a link to an online demo or a video of the application to be presented in the submission. Videos should be hosted on a web platform. Unlisted links are entirely acceptable to prevent indexing by search engines. Video may be up to one minute in length to showcase the application's maturity. Voiceovers are not necessary, and simple screen recordings captured using standard video conferencing software are perfectly acceptable. Please note that videos are required only for the review process and may be removed once notifications have been issued.
7.  **Maturity indicator**. A 1- or 2-line statement of development stage (prototype, production readiness).
8.  **Data sources used**. Given the semantics focus, a short line on which KGs/ontologies/standards the system builds on, if any.

EasyChair submission link: <https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=semantics2026>

## Evaluation Process and Eligibility

Proposals will be evaluated by the IE committee based on:

  * Quality of description and demo video.
  * Originality and relevance to the broader semantics field (alignment with the conference theme is a plus, but not strictly required).
  * Maturity and demonstrated functionality of the system.
  * Logistical considerations for on-site presentation.

Commercial products are eligible, but proposals focused purely on sales/marketing are not appropriate. Demonstrations of previously published systems are welcome if they offer a new perspective or focus relevant to SEMANTiCS 2026.

## Publication Note

Please note that the Practitioners Track is non archival, hence all accepted contributions will not be included in the conference proceedings. However, they will be featured on the SEMANTiCS Website.

## Registration Information

Being a live demonstration to the SEMANTiCS 2026 participants, each accepted Practitioner's Track contribution must be accompanied by at least one registered author. A maximum of two demos per registered author is allowed. **Authors are expected to be present on-site in Ghent** to interact with the audience during the Practitioners Track sessions.
